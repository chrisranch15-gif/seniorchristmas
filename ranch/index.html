Why we do this

We believe everyone deserves to be noticed. When we started in 2015, the wish lists were heartbreakingly simple, like a pair of socks or a bag of Cheetos. Many of these seniors no longer get visits from family, and that's what turned a one-time idea into a tradition we'll never skip.

Each year we invite our neighbors, friends and their kids to join us in giving, because looking out for one another isn't just worth talking about. It's worth doing. And every year, it ends up being the best part of our Christmas.

— The Ranch Real Estate Group
